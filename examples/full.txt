# Acme Robotics

> Acme Robotics builds open-source autonomous mobile robots for warehouse logistics, with deployments at 40+ distribution centers across North America.

Acme Robotics was founded in 2021 by a team from Boston Dynamics and Amazon Robotics. Headquartered in Pittsburgh, PA, with engineering offices in Boulder, CO and Toronto, ON.

## What Acme does

- **Autonomous mobile robots (AMRs)** for warehouse pick-pack-ship operations
- **Fleet orchestration software** that coordinates 100+ robots per facility
- **Open-source SDK** for partners to extend the platform
